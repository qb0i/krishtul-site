Holocaust Memorial

In July 1941, the Nazi and Romanian occupation reached Mohyliv-Podilskyi. The Krishtul family endured the ghetto, then were sent to the Pechora death camp. Raya Krishtul lost her daughters Bella and Maya there — they died of hunger, cold, and disease. She escaped by climbing the camp fence with her toddler son Vladimir. Israel Leib, Raya's father-in-law and a tailor by trade, survived by obtaining a sewing machine and making suits; locals helped him escape the camp and he later repaid them. Her husband Lev served at the front and survived the war, only to be killed accidentally in 1947.
